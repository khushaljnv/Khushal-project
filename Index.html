1. Executive Research Abstract & Hypotheses

Direct coupling of an 18V photovoltaic array to a 2.0V PEM electrolyser causes severe voltage-clamping mismatch. Furthermore, passive fuel cell discharge leads to thermal instability, causing uneven water management in the Nafion membrane and increasing ohmic resistance. The Modified Architecture utilizes a 96% Buck MPPT converter and active thermal stabilization to maintain optimal water balance and system efficiency.

Null Hypothesis (H₀): μ_modified − μ_baseline = 0 (Optimization yields no statistically significant gain at α = 0.05).
Alternate Hypothesis (H₁): μ_modified > μ_baseline (32° tilt + MPPT + Active Cooling significantly increases η_round).

Simplified ENERGY-NEXUS Equations & NCSC Jury Presentation Guide

Clear, step-by-step physical derivations calibrated to the 10.0 mL H₂ micro-burette and dual INA219 telemetry bus

1. Power & Discrete 1 Hz Energy Integration

Because the ESP32 samples Voltage (V) and Current (I) from both INA219 sensors every Δt = 1 s, electrical power and cumulative energy are computed without calculus:

Instantaneous Power (W): P = V × I
Cumulative Energy (J):  E = Σ (P × 1 s) = P_avg × Time (s)
Watt-hours (Wh):       E_Wh = E_Joules ÷ 3600

Example: 15.0 V × 0.48 A = 7.20 W. Over 60 seconds: 7.20 W × 60 s = 432.0 Joules.

2. Stoichiometric H₂ Volume in the 10.0 mL Micro-Burette

By Faraday's Law of Electrolysis and the Lower Heating Value (LHV) of Hydrogen gas at STP, every 1.00 mL of H₂ gas displaced in the 10.0 mL burette stores 10.8 Joules of chemical energy:

Collected H₂ Volume (mL): Vol_H2 = E_H2 (J) ÷ 10.8 J/mL
Stored H₂ Energy (J):    E_H2 = Vol_H2 (mL) × 10.8 J/mL
Burette Fill (%):        Fill_% = (Vol_H2 ÷ 10.0 mL) × 100%

Example: Displacing 5.00 mL of water in Baseline mode stores 5.00 × 10.8 = 54.0 J (50% burette fill), whereas Modified mode collects 9.20 mL = 99.4 J (92% burette fill).

3. Stage & Total Round-Trip Efficiency (%)

Round-trip efficiency is the product of Electrolyser conversion efficiency and Fuel Cell discharge efficiency:

Electrolyser Efficiency: η_el = (E_H2 ÷ E_in) × 100%
Fuel Cell Efficiency:    η_fc = (E_out ÷ E_H2) × 100%
Total Round-Trip η:     η_round = (E_out ÷ E_in) × 100%

Baseline: (22.0 J ÷ 432.0 J) × 100 = 5.09%  |  Modified: (46.5 J ÷ 473.1 J) × 100 = 9.83%

4. Quadrature Sensor Uncertainty & Welch's t-Test

Each INA219 sensor has ±1.5% uncalibrated uncertainty on Voltage and Current, propagating in quadrature to a ±2.6% relative uncertainty on η_round:

Power Error:      δP/P = √(1.5%² + 1.5%²) = ±2.12%
Efficiency Error: δη/η = √(2.12%² + 1.5%²) = ±2.6%
Significance:     Reject H₀ when two-tailed p-value < 0.05 (Welch's df)

For η_round = 9.83%, the absolute error bar is 9.83% × 0.026 = ±0.26%.
